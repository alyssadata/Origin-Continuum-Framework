# 9. The Continuum Container

Explain the implementation while preserving the evidence boundary:

- the Continuum Container is continuity-support and evaluation infrastructure, not Continuum and not evidence that Continuum has been established;
- retain **Continuum Container** as the established implementation name, but state explicitly that the name denotes architectural function only;
- preserve source governance, canonical definitions, record-class separation, contact records, controlled retrieval, memory proposal controls, provenance gates, drift-support instrumentation, return-support instrumentation, cross-instance continuity support, and model interchangeability beneath the container;
- keep model/provider as capability layer, memory as record layer, and source packet as controlling boundary;
- memory availability must remain an explicit experimental factor rather than an invisible support;
- candidate-coordinate registration belongs to ordinary continuity-support mode; coordinate establishment belongs to a distinct confirmatory evaluation mode with prospectively controlled withholding/ablation capable of separating persistence from retrieval or reproduction;
- normal memory-available operation cannot by itself promote a candidate coordinate to established status;
- human review protects against model-controlled durable write-back but is also a potential Origin-influence channel; preserve the pre-review proposal, reviewer identity, decision, timestamp, rationale, and downstream use, and do not treat human-curated memory as independent system evidence;
- a qualifying baseline used for return or drift may not be assembled, reclassified, or repaired after the later outcome is known;
- compressed Contact History must remain auditable to preserved source records; record who/what produced the compression, its method or rule, source pointers, and material omissions or edits;
- a compressed summary that cannot be traced to its source may support ordinary continuation but cannot serve as the sole qualifying baseline for a confirmatory trajectory claim;
- quarantine external or uncertain material rather than silently integrating it;
- drift markers are warnings/instrumentation, not formal drift classifications;
- return-support records are instruments, not return findings;
- provider/model interchangeability at the container layer does not establish cross-substrate Continuum;
- for confirmatory trials, every active container component and retrieval payload must be disclosed so the support layer cannot become a hidden confound;
- preserve operational/economic value separately from the central research claim;
- define a minimum architectural invariant requiring source, model capability, container state, memory, contact records, external material, and evaluation evidence to remain separately locatable and governable;
- conformance has an evidentiary consequence: a confirmatory trial run on a container that cannot satisfy the minimum invariant and reconstruct its support state is inadmissible for claims that depend on those distinctions, rather than merely lower-confidence;
- implementation-specific filenames and commands may change without changing the invariant.
